Focus Grappling – Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · App: Focus Grappling (Android, package com.focusgrappling.app)

In short: your training log is stored only on your phone. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the developer) never receive your data. If you turn on health sync, data moves only between Focus Grappling and Health Connect on your own device, and only with your permission.

1. Who we are

Focus Grappling is an independent app made by Paul Dunne ("we", "us"). Contact: Focus Grappling Support.

2. Data the app stores (on your device only)

You enter this yourself. It is kept in a database inside the app on your phone:

  • Training sessions: date, start time, duration, session type, gi/no-gi, sparring rounds, intensity and notes.
  • Techniques you tag, and submissions landed or caught in sparring.
  • Belt and stripe promotions and their dates.
  • Settings: weekly goal, body weight (used only to estimate calories), and whether Pro and health sync are on.

This data is never uploaded to us or to any server. The only network use in the app is Google Play checking your Pro subscription. Android's own device backup may include app data if you have turned on backup for your phone.

3. Health Connect (optional, Pro)

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Focus Grappling asks for these permissions. You can grant or refuse each one:

PermissionWhy
Write exerciseSave each training session you log as a martial-arts workout, so it appears in your other fitness apps (e.g. Samsung Health).
Write total calories burnedSave an estimated calorie burn for each session, calculated from duration, intensity and your body weight.
Read heart rateShow the average and maximum heart rate your watch recorded during each session.
Read exerciseFind workouts your watch recorded (e.g. a Martial Arts workout on an Amazfit or Galaxy Watch), so you can log them in Focus Grappling with one tap.

Health data read from Health Connect is used only to show these features to you inside the app. It is stored only on your device, never sent to us or third parties, never sold, and never used for advertising, credit or insurance decisions. We do not allow humans to read it.

Use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time in Android Settings › Health Connect › App permissions › Focus Grappling. Records Focus Grappling wrote stay in Health Connect until you delete them there.

4. Purchases

Pro subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your card details. The app only asks Google Play whether your subscription is active.

5. Sharing and exports

We do not share or sell any data. If you use CSV export (Pro), the file is handed to the Android share sheet and goes only where you choose to send it.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete any session in the app (Journal › session › Delete).
  • Delete everything: Android Settings › Apps › Focus Grappling › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Revoke health access: Android Settings › Health Connect.

Because we never hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. There is no account to close.

7. Children

Focus Grappling is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

8. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's lock screen.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
